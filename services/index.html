★ The Barbers

  • Marcus Hayes

    Master Barber & Founder · Since 2009

    Fades · Straight Razor Shaves

    Marcus spent ten years in Chicago shops before opening Noble & Sharp in 2009. He is known for fades so clean they look painted on, and for a hot-towel shave he will not rush.

  • Darnell Croft

    Senior Barber · Since 2014

    Skin Fades · Beard Sculpting

    Darnell is a Chicago South Side native who discovered barbering at 16 and never looked back. His skin fades are architectural — he approaches every cut like a sculptor reading the head before making a single move.

  • Tommy Reyes

    Barber · Since 2018

    Classic Cuts · Textured Styles

    Tommy brings a love of vintage men's grooming to every appointment. He loves old-school pomades and a sharp side part, and is just as happy giving a modern textured crop. His executive cuts are a neighbourhood favourite.

  • Elijah Cross

    Barber · Since 2021

    Creative Designs · Tapered Cuts

    The youngest member of the team, Elijah joined straight from barber school with a passion for creative freehand design work. He blends old-school fundamentals with fresh barbering energy that keeps the younger clientele coming back.
