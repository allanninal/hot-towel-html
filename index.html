Good to Know

Shop Policies

Cancellation
Please cancel or move an appointment at least 24 hours ahead, by phone, email or your booking page. There is no charge.
No-shows
If you cancel with less than 24 hours' notice or do not come, we may ask for half the price of the booked service at your next visit.
Running late
We hold your chair for 10 minutes. After that we fit you in as soon as we can, shorten the service to keep the next client on time, or move you to another day.
Kids' cuts
The Kids' Cut is for children under 12 and takes 30 minutes. A parent or guardian stays in the shop. Book ahead on Saturdays.
Walk-ins
Walk-ins are always welcome based on barber availability. For a guaranteed chair, especially on weekends, call or email us at least a day ahead.

Our Story

Built on Old-School Values

Noble & Sharp opened on North Wells Street in 2009 with a single chair, a vintage barber sign, and one guiding belief: a great haircut is not a commodity. It is a craft — one that demands patience, study, and an honest conversation with every man who sits down.

Founder Marcus Hayes had spent a decade honing his scissor work and straight-razor technique under Chicago's best, and he opened Noble & Sharp as a place where the old rituals still matter. The hot towel. The hot lather. The unhurried shave. The conversation that makes the chair feel like the most comfortable seat in the city.

Today, four barbers carry that philosophy forward — each with their own specialty, each committed to sending every client out the door looking sharper than when they arrived.

We don't cut corners. We sharpen them.
