Meet Your Barbers

Since 2009

Marcus Hayes

Master Barber & Founder

Fades · Straight Razor Shaves

Marcus spent ten years in Chicago shops before opening Noble & Sharp in 2009. He is known for fades so clean they look painted on, and for a hot-towel shave he will not rush.

Since 2014

Darnell Croft

Senior Barber

Skin Fades · Beard Sculpting

Darnell is a Chicago South Side native who discovered barbering at 16 and never looked back. His skin fades are architectural — he approaches every cut like a sculptor reading the head before making a single move.

Since 2018

Tommy Reyes

Barber

Classic Cuts · Textured Styles

Tommy brings a love of vintage men's grooming to every appointment. He loves old-school pomades and a sharp side part, and is just as happy giving a modern textured crop. His executive cuts are a neighbourhood favourite.

Since 2021

Elijah Cross

Barber

Creative Designs · Tapered Cuts

The youngest member of the team, Elijah joined straight from barber school with a passion for creative freehand design work. He blends old-school fundamentals with fresh barbering energy that keeps the younger clientele coming back.

Our Story

Built on Old-School Values

Noble & Sharp opened on North Wells Street in 2009 with a single chair, a vintage barber sign, and one guiding belief: a great haircut is not a commodity. It is a craft — one that demands patience, study, and an honest conversation with every man who sits down.

Founder Marcus Hayes had spent a decade honing his scissor work and straight-razor technique under Chicago's best, and he opened Noble & Sharp as a place where the old rituals still matter. The hot towel. The hot lather. The unhurried shave. The conversation that makes the chair feel like the most comfortable seat in the city.

Today, four barbers carry that philosophy forward — each with their own specialty, each committed to sending every client out the door looking sharper than when they arrived.

We don't cut corners. We sharpen them.
