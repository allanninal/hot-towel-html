What happens in a hot-towel shave

The shave starts before the razor comes out. You lie back in the chair and we wrap your face in a hot towel for a few minutes, which softens the beard and makes the whole shave smoother. Then we work in a warm lather with a brush.

We shave with a straight razor, first with the grain and then, if your skin is happy with it, a second pass across it. It is slow on purpose. Tell us straight away if anything stings or pulls, and let us know beforehand if your skin reacts to products or you have shaved in the last day.

A cold towel closes the shave, followed by a light balm. Most people book it on a Friday or before an event. If you want the full routine, the Beard & Cut Combo adds a haircut, and gift cards cover the shave too.
